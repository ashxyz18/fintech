The engine

10 strategies. 2-strategy confluence. Zero emotion.

Every trade requires at least two independent strategies to agree on direction, the regime to be favorable, and fundamentals not to conflict. Anything less = no trade.

Trend-ADX

EMA20 / EMA50 cross filtered by ADX > 22. Only acts in established trends, never in chop.

Order-flow imbalance

Reads the bid/ask aggressor ratio. Enters only when buyers (or sellers) are clearly dominant.

MACD + Volume (OBV)

MACD breakouts only count when on-balance-volume confirms — no chasing thin moves.

VWAP pullback

Institutional traders defend VWAP. We buy retests in uptrends, fade them in downtrends.

Liquidity sweep

Detects "stop hunts" — when price spikes through a swing high then reverses — and trades the reversal.

Order block (SMC)

Identifies the last opposite candle before a strong impulse, then trades the retest.

Supply & demand

Maps swing zones that previously produced strong moves and trades the retest with ATR-sized risk.

Trendline break

Connects last two swings — buys the break of falling lines, sells the break of rising ones.

Engulfing

Bullish/bearish engulfing patterns at swing extremes — classic price-action reversal.

Bollinger reversion

Mean reversion at band extremes when ADX is low — fades exhaustion in ranging markets.

FAQ

Common questions.

How do you execute trades on real exchanges?
We connect via institutional APIs (Binance, Coinbase Prime, Interactive Brokers, OANDA, CME) using our prime-brokerage relationships. Orders are smart-routed for best execution.
Are my funds safe?
Cash sits in FDIC-insured partner banks. Crypto is held in qualified custody (Anchorage / BitGo) with multi-sig and 95% cold storage. We never have unilateral access to your funds.
What's the minimum to invest?
$50 for most funds. $250 for Breakout Pro and Smart Money funds (which use larger position sizes).
How fast can I withdraw?
Crypto: 2–10 minutes. USDT: instant. Bank wire: 1–2 business days. Debit card: ~30 minutes. No lock-ups.
What's the difference between Main and Trading wallets?
Main is your withdrawable cash. Trading is the capital allocated to AI funds. Move freely between them — instant, zero fees.
Can the AI lose my money?
Yes — trading carries risk. We mitigate with tight per-trade stop-losses (1%), a -5% daily circuit breaker, regime gates, and 3:1 reward-to-risk targets. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results.
